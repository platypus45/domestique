Fatigue Resistance — robustness index (beta)
Threshold:
i
THRESHOLD: > 1500 kJ (default)
The conventional "durability ride" inclusion threshold used in van Erp et al. 2021 (Visma retrospective), Maturana et al. 2025 review, and Mateo-March et al. 2022 (IJSPP 17:6). Captures meaningful glycogen depletion onset (~1800–2200 kJ for trained cyclists per Coyle 1986). Wide adoption in performance research and the broadest sample size for most amateur riders.
THRESHOLD: > 2000 kJ (strict Pinot 2014)
The original Pinot & Grappe 2014 measurement point — the threshold at which trained-cyclist glycogen depletion approaches its peak, derived from Grand Tour stage data (3500–4500 kJ per stage) and validated against post-stage power retention. The gold standard for elite-rider durability assessment. Smaller N for amateurs, but the most rigorous comparison against the WorldTour 92–95% robustness benchmark.

How your plan updates
Update plan is the one button you need — it automatically does the right thing: rebalances load to today's fitness and fatigue, or, if you've missed enough training to fall behind, rebuilds with a safe recovery ramp (no catch-up spike). It also runs automatically after every ride sync, so your plan stays current on its own; the status line shows what changed. Rebuild from scratch is the advanced option that wipes and rebuilds the plan from today's CTL — use it after a goal or event-date change. The Availability Calendar edits which days you can train; click a day to set hours, right-click to mark unavailable, then press the pulsing UPDATE button to reflow. Rematch workout (in any day's detail view) re-rolls only that single workout at the same duration if the suggestion doesn't fit. Don't manually edit unless something's clearly wrong — the planner already accounts for fatigue and load.
Reschedule missed sessions?
Update plan automatically picks the right adjustment: a structure-preserving rebalance to today's TSB (fatigue), ACWR (load ratio) and availability when you're on track, or a full rebuild with a recovery ramp when you've missed enough to fall behind (Gabbett-safe, never a catch-up spike). Runs automatically after each ride sync too. Won't re-roll past or completed sessions, and won't move a fixed event date.
Reforecast keeps your plan's structure but rebalances daily load to today's TSB (fatigue), ACWR (load ratio), and polarization compliance. Now folded into Update plan.
Rebuild from scratch wipes the plan and rebuilds it from today's CTL, with phase splits derived from weeks-to-event. Use after a goal change or new event date. This is the advanced/nuclear option — Update plan already rebuilds automatically when a training gap warrants it.
Edit availability sets per-day training time. Click a date to set hours; right-click to mark unavailable (holiday/injury). Press the pulsing UPDATE button to apply changes — the plan reflows on click.
Rematch workout re-rolls only this one day's ZWO file from the matching workout pool. Same intent (intervals, endurance, etc.) — different specific session. Use when the suggested workout doesn't fit your equipment or mood.
