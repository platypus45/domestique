Activity Sync

Intervals.icu gets new activities automatically from Garmin Connect (via its API). Connect Garmin now so rides appear in your plan automatically.

Garmin Connect sync (required):
  1. In Intervals.icu → Settings → Connections
  2. Enable Garmin Connect as your activity source

This ensures Intervals.icu gets new data automatically via Garmin's API.
