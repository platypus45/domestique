Domestique

Let's get you set up in 4 quick steps.

Connect your data

Domestique reads your training data (fitness, fatigue, form, HRV, activities) from Intervals.icu — a free training-analysis service. You connect by signing in on intervals.icu in your browser — no API keys to copy.

1 · Intervals.icu account

No account yet? It's free — create one at intervals.icu. You can sign in with your Garmin or Strava account (one click, no new password).

2 · Connect — one click, no keys

✨ Sign in to intervals.icu — it opens in your browser, you log in and approve once, and we link that account here (no API keys to copy). Optional now; you can also connect later in Settings. Until then, fitness (CTL/ATL/TSB) is computed from any FIT files you import.

3 · Garmin Connect → Intervals.icu

So new rides appear in your plan automatically, let Intervals.icu pull them from Garmin Connect (one-time):

  1. Open intervals.icu/settings → Connections
  2. Click Garmin Connect → authorise (one-click OAuth)
New rides can take a few minutes to appear, and the connection is region-specific — if it fails, retry from a desktop browser.
